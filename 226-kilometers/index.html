226 Kilometers

An Ironman is 3.8 kilometers swimming, 180 on the bike and 42.2 running, in one day. The body is the easy part.

An Ironman is 3.8 kilometers of swimming, 180 kilometers on a bike and a full marathon, 42.2 kilometers, all in one day. I am training for my first.

Everyone asks about the body. The body is the easy part. You train it, and it adapts. The hard part is the conversation in your head at kilometer thirty, when every cell is asking you to stop.

Discipline is freedom

The Stoics understood this two thousand years ago. You do not control the heat, the wind or the cramp in your leg. You control your response. Training is not really about fitness. It is about rehearsing that response until it becomes who you are.

The plan you follow when it stops being fun

Every endurance athlete learns the same lesson: motivation gets you to the start line, a plan gets you to the finish. Some days the plan is boring. Some days it hurts. You follow it anyway, because you made it when you were thinking clearly.

The limit is usually a story in your head. Stories can be rewritten.

It is the same lesson as trading, the same lesson as building a company. I just learned it with my legs.
