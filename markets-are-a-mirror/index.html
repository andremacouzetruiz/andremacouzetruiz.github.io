Markets Are a Mirror

Every chart is a record of fear and greed. Trading is the best psychology lab I have found.

People think trading is about predicting prices. After years of trading Nasdaq futures, I think it is mostly about predicting yourself.

Every candle on a chart is a record of thousands of people being afraid or greedy at the same moment. The market does not care about your analysis. It reflects back, with perfect accuracy, every weakness you bring to it.

The real opponent

The trades that cost me the most were never the ones where I was wrong about the market. They were the ones where I knew the rule and broke it anyway: one more trade after a loss, a stop moved because it felt personal, a winner closed early because I could not stand to give anything back.

Rules as promises

A written rule is a promise to your future self, made while you are calm, for the moment when you will not be. That is why at Northpoint we build tools that score every trade against the trader's own rules. Not to judge, but to show the pattern.

The market is a mirror. The question is whether you are willing to look.

Better traders make better decisions. I suspect the same is true of better people.
