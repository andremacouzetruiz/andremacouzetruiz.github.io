01 · About

Measure first, believe second.

I built the backtest that proved my own trading strategy lost money. Then I changed the strategy.

That is the whole method. A model on a laptop changes nothing, so I take an idea from raw data to a deployed product, and then I measure whether it worked. When the number is uncomfortable, I keep the number.

I started my first business at twelve and ran retail and distribution in Mexico City until 2021. Today I build software: trading tools at Northpoint, landing pages and apps at Studio AMR, and products of my own.
