I am building
toward a calmer,
smarter world.

Today feels like a noisy office on fire: too much information, too little thinking, cities designed for cars and minds designed for distraction. I don't think it has to stay that way. These are the futures I believe in, and the ones I am working on.

Postcards
from the future.

what I want to see in my lifetime
2035

Cities built for humans, not cars.

Northpoint Cities starts with Anthropos City on the coast of Azuero: a round plaza, eight avenues in a star, radii that grow by the golden ratio, everything within a fifteen-minute walk. Architecture as a tool for better lives.

anthropos = human being
2030

Machines that think with us.

AI agents as colleagues, not replacements: they read, test and remember, so people can spend their time on judgment, taste and care.

I build with them every day
2040

The mind, finally understood.

Psychoanalysis meets neuroscience halfway. We stop treating anxiety as weakness and start reading it as information. Freud asked the right questions; this century answers them.

2027

Bodies that go further.

My own near future: an Ironman. 226 kilometers in a day. Proof that the limit is usually a story in your head, and that stories can be rewritten.

start line, 2027
2032

Markets without fear.

Trading as a discipline of rules and self-knowledge, not adrenaline. Northpoint exists to make better traders and better decisions.

2050

Reality, stranger than we thought.

Quantum computers, entanglement, the observer problem. The deepest layer of the world behaves nothing like the surface, and the next breakthroughs will come from people willing to accept that.

the most beautiful fact I know
always

Leave it better built than you found it.

In 1826 Jean-François Macouzet crossed the ocean from Lyon and helped found institutional medicine in Michoacán. Two centuries later I want to do the same with cities, software and minds: build something that outlasts me.

How I think
about everything.

01

The map is not the territory.

Every model is a simplification. Test it against reality, rewrite it when reality wins.

02

Measure first, believe second.

An uncomfortable number beats a beautiful story.

03

Everything flows.

Markets, cities and people are processes, not things. Design for change.

04

Discipline is freedom.

You control your response, not the event. A written rule is a promise to your future self.

05

We are not that rational.

Most of what moves us happens below awareness. Know it, and you can work with it.

06

Places shape people.

We build our cities, and then our cities build us.
