01 · How I see the worldManifesto

The map is not the territory.

Every model we carry, about money, about people, about ourselves, is a simplification. I spend my life testing mine against reality and rewriting them when reality wins.

I

Measure first, believe second.

What goes unmeasured gets told however we like. I would rather keep an uncomfortable number than a beautiful story.

II

We are not as rational as we think.

Most of what drives us happens below awareness. Freud saw it in dreams, Kahneman in experiments, I see it in every trade log.

III

Everything flows.

Heraclitus said you never step into the same river twice. Markets, cities and people are processes, not things. Design for change.

IV

Discipline is a form of freedom.

The Stoics had it right: you control your response, not the event. A written rule is a promise to your future self.

V

Cities shape the people inside them.

We build our places, and then our places build us. Architecture is psychology at the scale of a street.

VI

Reality is stranger than intuition.

A single particle can interfere with itself. If the universe does not fit common sense, neither will the best ideas.

03 · The world, for me

An unfinished
city.

The world is not a fixed place we inherit. It is something we keep building, street by street, rule by rule, mind by mind. Science tells me how it works, philosophy asks what it is for, psychoanalysis shows me why I resist it, and endurance teaches me to keep going anyway.
