Thinking With Machines

AI should not replace our judgment. It should give us more time to use it.

I build with AI agents every day. They read documents, write code, test ideas and remember what I forget. They have changed how I work more than anything since I learned to program.

And yet the most important thing they have taught me is what they cannot do for me.

Leverage, not replacement

An agent can test a thousand strategies overnight. It cannot decide which question is worth asking. It can draft a city plan. It cannot decide what a good life inside that city looks like. Taste, judgment and care are still ours.

The danger is not that machines will think. It is that we will stop. If we outsource the questions along with the answers, we become passengers in our own lives.

The partnership

The future I want is one where machines handle the repetition and humans handle the meaning. Where a small studio can build what used to require a hundred people, and a single founder can test ideas at the speed of thought.

Machines that think with us, not for us.

That future is not automatic. It has to be designed, like everything else that matters.
