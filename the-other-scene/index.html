The Other Scene

Freud called the unconscious the other scene. It is where most of our decisions are actually made.

Freud called the unconscious the other scene: a stage behind the stage, where the real play happens while we watch the one we think we are in.

I started reading psychoanalysis to understand my trading. I kept reading because it explained almost everything else.

Why we repeat

We like to believe we are rational. Then we watch ourselves make the same mistake for the fifth time, in markets, in relationships, in work, and we cannot explain why. Psychoanalysis has an answer: what we do not remember, we repeat.

Kahneman found the same thing in experiments. Most of what moves us happens below awareness, fast and automatic. The conscious mind arrives later and writes the explanation.

Working with it

This is not a reason for despair. It is a reason for humility, and for better design. If you know where the other scene is, you can build systems, rules and environments that work with it instead of against it.

Know what moves you, and you can choose what to do with it.

I think the next great frontier is not outer space. It is the mind we live inside every day and still barely understand.
