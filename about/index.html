André Macouzet

I am the founder of Northpoint, where we work on trading psychology, behavioral finance and technology, and of Studio AMR, a software studio that designs and builds landing pages, apps and trading systems.

I also work on Northpoint Cities, starting with Anthropos City: a new city on the coast of Azuero planned around people instead of cars.

I write about technology, cities, markets and the human mind. I read psychoanalysis and philosophy, follow physics with more enthusiasm than expertise, trade Nasdaq futures, play golf and am training for my first Ironman.

My family name comes from Grigny, near Lyon. In 1826 Jean-François Macouzet crossed the ocean and helped found institutional medicine in Michoacán. I live in Morelia, México.

You can write to me at andremacouzetruiz@gmail.com.
